Meet Campus Copilot

Campus Copilot is an AI-powered student productivity assistant designed to eliminate repetitive form filling and simplify everyday campus tasks.

Built for students, by students.

❌ The Problem

College life involves an endless cycle of entering the exact same identity, contact, academic, and parent details over and over again on:

  • Google Forms & registration links
  • College event registrations & TechFests
  • Wi-Fi/Mess review & feedback checklists
  • Scholarship and internship application drafts

🚀 The Solution

Campus Copilot securely stores your student information locally. Using Gemini API semantic mapping, it matches custom text fields to corresponding profile data instantly.

  • 100% Client-Side Encryption
  • Synonym resolution & confidence checkmarks
  • Dictate and auto-generate text fields
  • Always keeps you in control before submission
